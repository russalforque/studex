Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]. Studex is operated by [BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS], Philippines ("we").

The short version

  • Everything you put into the Studex app (classes, grades, notes, files, allowance, expenses, savings) stays on your phone. We never receive it.
  • To sell and license Studex we keep your email, your order and payment status, and a record of which devices activated your license.
  • We don't use ads, trackers or analytics, and we never sell your data.

What we collect, and why

  • Purchase: your email address, the amount, date and payment status, and the payment method type (e.g. GCash). Payment details (card numbers, wallet accounts) are handled by PayMongo and never reach us. Purpose: to deliver and support your purchase, and to meet accounting and tax obligations.
  • License activation: a random installation ID made by the app, a public key made by the app, a one-way hash of an app-specific device identifier (to recognise a reinstall on the same phone), the phone model and Android version, and the Studex version. Purpose: to enforce the device limit and let you move your license. We don't collect IMEI, phone numbers, contacts or location.
  • Security logs: a keyed hash of your IP address (not the address itself) and the time of activation, restore and download requests. Purpose: preventing abuse such as guessing license codes.
  • Support emails you send us.

Who we share it with

  • PayMongo (payment processing), Cloudflare (hosting and database) and our email provider (sending your license and verification codes), only as needed to provide these services.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Purchase and license records: for as long as your license exists and as required by Philippine tax rules. Verification codes: deleted after 30 days. Rate-limit counters: deleted after 2 days.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its processing. Email support@studex.example. Deleting your purchase record means we can no longer restore your license.

The app

The Studex app connects to the internet only when you activate, restore or move your license, or when you tap "Check license status". Backups you make are files you control; we never see them.
