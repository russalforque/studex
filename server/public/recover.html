Restore your purchase

Already bought Studex? You never need to pay again.

Reinstalled Studex, or got a new phone?

Open Studex, tap Restore purchase and enter your purchase email. We send a 6-digit code to confirm it's you. You don't need your license code or your old phone.

Lost your license code?

Enter your purchase email. We'll send a one-time link that gives you a new code. The old code stops working; phones already using Studex keep working.

Your data is separate from your license

Restoring your purchase unlocks Studex. Your classes, notes and money records live on your phone, not with us. To move them, make a backup in Settings → Data on the old phone and restore it on the new one. If a phone is lost without a backup, its data can't be recovered.

Your new license code

This makes a new code for your purchase and turns the old one off. Phones already using Studex keep working.

Write it down or keep it in a password manager. Open Studex, tap Activate Studex and enter it.
