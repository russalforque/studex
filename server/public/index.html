Questions

Is it really a one-time payment?

Yes. You pay once and keep Studex. There's no subscription, and updates are included.

Do I need the internet?

Only once, to activate. After that Studex never checks online and works with no connection for as long as you use it.

What if I uninstall Studex or change phones?

Install it again, tap Restore purchase and enter your purchase email. We send a 6-digit code to confirm it's you. You don't need the old phone, and you never pay again.

Will my classes and budget come back too?

Only if you have a backup. Your data lives on your phone, not on our servers. Use Settings → Data → Back up regularly and keep the file somewhere safe (Google Drive, email). Restore it after activating on the new phone. If a phone is lost without a backup, its data can't be recovered.

How many devices can I use?

One device at a time. You can move your license to a new phone yourself several times a year; if you need more, contact support and we'll help.

I lost my license code.

Use Recover license. We email a one-time link that gives you a new code. Phones already using Studex keep working.

Is it safe to install an app from a website?

Studex is signed with our release key, and every version lists its SHA-256 checksum so you can verify the file. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser once; see the installation guide.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 7 days of purchase if Studex doesn't work for you. See the Refund Policy. A refunded license stops working.

Need help?

Email support@studex.example. Include the email you bought Studex with. We reply within 2 working days.
